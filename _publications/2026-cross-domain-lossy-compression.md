This paper studies cross-domain lossy compression through the lens of minimum entropy coupling (MEC) with rate and classification constraints. An encoder observes samples from a degraded source domain while the decoder must generate outputs following a prescribed target distribution and preserve information relevant to a downstream classification task. We formulate a rate-constrained MEC problem, show an equivalent deterministic coupling representation, and derive closed-form Bernoulli characterizations with and without classification constraints, implemented via a neural restoration framework.
