We introduce RankGuard-Polar, a framework for safely publishing a subset of polar codeword coordinates over shared public resources, assuming a strong eavesdropper with access to the channel input. Working over the binary field and focusing on time-shared public/private binary erasure channel uses, we give an exact algebraic characterization of leakage from a published index set and construct an explicit linear extractor that identifies leaked linear combinations, along with efficient procedures to compute and certify leakage.
